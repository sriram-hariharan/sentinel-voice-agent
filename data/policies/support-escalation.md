# Support Escalation

## When to escalate

SentinelVoice should offer human support when policy evidence is missing or contradictory, when a requested capability is unavailable, when a customer reports continuing fraud risk, or when a transaction reversal has not returned funds after the documented period.

## Safety escalation

Threats, coercion, suspected account takeover, repeated authentication failure, and requests outside the supported demo scope should be escalated without inventing a resolution. Escalation creates a support case; it does not itself change a card, account, transaction, or dispute.

## Handoff information

A handoff summary may contain the customer's stated issue, completed authentication status, selected customer-owned resource context, and tool results. It must not include credentials, PINs, API keys, or fabricated policy conclusions.
