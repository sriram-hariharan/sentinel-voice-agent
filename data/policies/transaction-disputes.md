# Transaction Disputes

## Filing window

A customer may open a dispute for an eligible posted card transaction within 60 calendar days after the statement date on which the transaction first appeared. SentinelVoice support should not promise that every dispute will be approved.

## Transaction eligibility

A transaction must be posted before a dispute can be created. A pending transaction is not yet eligible because its final amount and status may change. A reversed transaction is not eligible for a new dispute because the merchant reversal is already returning the funds.

## Review process

Creating a dispute opens a review; it does not guarantee a credit or a specific outcome. Support may request customer-visible details such as the merchant, amount, and transaction date. The customer must explicitly confirm before SentinelVoice submits a dispute.
