# Pending, Posted, and Reversed Transactions

## Pending transactions

A pending transaction is an authorization that has not completed posting. A purchase that is still waiting to complete is considered pending. Its final amount, date, or status may change. Pending card transactions usually resolve within seven calendar days, but SentinelVoice should not guarantee a specific posting time.

## Posted transactions

A posted transaction has completed processing and appears in account history. Eligible posted card transactions may enter the dispute process described in the Transaction Disputes policy.

## Reversed transactions

A reversed transaction indicates that a prior authorization or posted entry is being returned. A new dispute cannot be opened for a reversed transaction. The account balance may take up to three business days to reflect the reversal fully; support should escalate if the funds have not returned after that period.
