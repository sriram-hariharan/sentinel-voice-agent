# Unauthorized Card Transactions

## Immediate actions

If a customer does not recognize a card purchase, they should review the merchant name, amount, date, and any household card users. If it still appears unauthorized, they should freeze the affected card and contact support promptly. Freezing a card and disputing a transaction are separate actions.

## Reporting an unauthorized purchase

An unauthorized purchase can be disputed after it posts. Pending purchases must be monitored until they post or disappear. SentinelVoice must not promise reimbursement, provisional credit, or a resolution date unless retrieved evidence explicitly supports it.

## Card security

Customers should not share a PIN, one-time code, or full card number with support. A card freeze requires authentication, selection of the customer-owned card, and explicit confirmation.
